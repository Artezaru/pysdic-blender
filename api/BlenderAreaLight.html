pysdic_blender.BlenderAreaLight#

class BlenderAreaLight(position, direction, *, size, size_y=None, shape='SQUARE', up=None, energy=1000.0, color=(1.0, 1.0, 1.0), spread=3.141592653589793)[source]#

Bases: object

An area light of Blender: a flat emitting surface (square, rectangle, disk or ellipse), e.g. an LED panel or a diffuser.

  • shape: "SQUARE" or "DISK" (one dimension size), "RECTANGLE" or "ELLIPSE" (dimensions size along the local \(x\) axis and size_y along the local \(y\) axis);

  • energy: the total power in watts emitted by the surface (Blender convention);

  • color: the linear RGB color of the light (white by default);

  • spread: the angle of the cone of emission of each point of the surface, in radians in \(]0, \pi]\) (\(\pi\): a Lambertian emitter; smaller: a directional panel, like a honeycomb grid).

The surface is centered on position and emits from one side only, along direction (the local \(-z\) axis of the Blender light). Its local \(y\) axis (along size_y) is up made orthogonal to direction: it orients a rectangle or an ellipse around the direction. The object is immutable.

Parameters:

  • position (ArrayLike) – The center \((3,)\) of the surface in the world.

  • direction (ArrayLike) – The direction \((3,)\) of emission, normal to the surface (normalized).

  • size (Real) – The side ("SQUARE", "RECTANGLE") or the diameter ("DISK", "ELLIPSE") along the local \(x\) axis, in the unit of the scene (positive).

  • size_y (Optional[Real], optional) – The dimension along the local \(y\) axis, required for "RECTANGLE" and "ELLIPSE", forbidden for "SQUARE" and "DISK".

  • shape (str, optional (default: "SQUARE")) – "SQUARE", "RECTANGLE", "DISK" or "ELLIPSE".

  • up (Optional[ArrayLike], optional) – The direction \((3,)\) of the local \(y\) axis of the surface (made orthogonal to direction, must not be parallel to it). By default, an arbitrary deterministic axis (see up): give it for a rectangle, an ellipse or a square whose orientation matters.

  • energy (Real, optional (default: 1000.0)) – The power in watts (non-negative).

  • color (ArrayLike, optional (default: (1.0, 1.0, 1.0))) – The RGB color \((3,)\), non-negative linear values.

  • spread (Real, optional (default: pi)) – The angle of emission in radians, in \(]0, \pi]\).

Examples

from pysdic_blender import BlenderAreaLight

panel = BlenderAreaLight.looking_at(
    [0.0, -300.0, 500.0], [0.0, 0.0, 0.0], size=200.0, size_y=100.0, shape="RECTANGLE",
    up=[1.0, 0.0, 0.0], energy=2e5,
)
